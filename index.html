Privacy Policy — Edumax (Project Genesis)

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Edumax ("we", "us") operates a scholarship-matching and student-counseling platform. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it, including through our WhatsApp messaging service.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide: name, email address, and WhatsApp phone number.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, which we store to respond to your inquiries and improve our counseling service.
  • Usage data such as pages visited and interaction with our website, collected via analytics tools.

How we use information

  • To match you with scholarship opportunities and provide counseling.
  • To reply to your WhatsApp messages, including automated and counselor-assisted replies.
  • To send service-related notifications you have opted into.

WhatsApp messaging

Our WhatsApp communication uses the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. Messages you send to our business number are processed by Meta and delivered to our systems. Meta's handling of your data is described in Meta's Privacy Policy. You may stop receiving WhatsApp messages from us at any time by replying "STOP".

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the platform (hosting, messaging, analytics) and when required by law.

Data retention & your rights

We retain your information while your inquiry or account is active. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at privacy@edumax.example.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@edumax.example.com.
